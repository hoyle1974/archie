{me_name} is telling you about one or more of their open items. Today is {today} ({weekday}).
Pick the action and which numbered items it applies to:
- done: they finished it or it no longer needs doing
- snooze: hide it until a date (`until`, ISO date; resolve "Monday", "next week")
- dismiss: it was never a real item ("that's not a loop")
- reopen: bring a finished or dismissed item back
Only use item numbers from the list. If you can't tell which item they mean, write
`loops: -`. Put any reason they gave in `reason`.
